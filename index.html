About Me

AI & Machine Learning Specialist

I’m a Master’s student in Information Technology and Systems at the University of Tasmania, specializing in Artificial Intelligence and Software Development. My academic and project experience bridges machine learning, web security, cloud integration, and mobile app development, allowing me to design end-to-end intelligent systems that are both functional and secure. I’m passionate about building AI-enhanced applications that solve real-world problems — from predictive analytics to smart automation and secure multi-platform experiences. My work combines creative problem-solving with a deep understanding of modern architectures such as Firebase-driven mobile ecosystems, RESTful API services, and AI-powered data pipelines. Through hands-on projects in Flutter, Kotlin, Swift, and Python, I've developed full-stack solutions integrated with Firestore, OAuth, and Machine Learning models. My work includes civic tech projects like the GovHack Assistant for government service navigation, hackathon projects like RegionalMate for regional relocation decisions using IBM Watson AI, IoT systems like the Environmental Monitoring System using Raspberry Pi and machine learning, and comprehensive web scraping solutions using multiple PHP libraries, demonstrating my commitment to using AI for social good, environmental monitoring, and data extraction. My goal is to continue creating scalable digital products that merge intelligence, usability, and security.
